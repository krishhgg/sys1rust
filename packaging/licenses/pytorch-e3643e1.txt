PyTorch, the source of MLX 0.32.2's float to fp8 e4m3 conversion (mlx/backend/metal/kernels/fp8.h
and mlx/backend/cpu/unary_ops.h) and of its CPU erf (mlx/backend/cpu/simd/math.h), in
lib/mlx.metallib and lib/libmlx.dylib, which also keeps them as source
Source: https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/blob/e3643e1e0e923f0fc063dfab6f45c956d568919d/LICENSE

abf28982a8cb43342e7669d859de9543fd804cc9, the commit MLX's CPU erf cites.
